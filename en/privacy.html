Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

1. Principle

This site is an independently run information site that publishes aggregated 4-star hotel prices for cities around the world. Protecting visitors’ personal information matters to us, and we handle it as set out below.

2. What we do not collect

The site consists of static web pages only. There is no sign-up, no contact form and no comment section. You are never asked to enter or send a name, address, telephone number or email address on this site.

3. Data stored in your browser (localStorage)

To keep the site convenient to use, the following is stored in your own browser:

KeyPurpose
favCountriesCountries you saved as favourites
favCitiesCities you saved as favourites
basemap2Your chosen map style (auto / standard / light / dark)
themeYour chosen appearance (auto / light / dark)
curYour chosen display currency
langYour chosen language
  • This data stays in your browser and is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • It contains nothing that identifies you.
  • You can clear it at any time by deleting this site’s data in your browser settings.

4. Analytics

The site is published on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record the IP address of visitors. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

If we ever add our own analytics tool, such as Google Analytics, we will update this page and say so explicitly.

5. Advertising

The site currently carries no advertising. If we later display ads from a third-party provider such as Google AdSense, that provider may use cookies to show ads based on your interests. We would then update this page with a link to Advertising – Policies and Terms – Google and instructions for opting out of personalised ads.

6. External services contacted

Displaying a page causes your browser to contact the following services. Those providers may record your IP address.

7. External links

This site links to external sites such as Booking.com. For how those sites handle personal information, please read their own privacy policies. We accept no liability for anything that happens on a linked site.

8. Changes

This policy may be revised without notice. A revision takes effect when it is published on this page.

9. Contact

Contact details are on the about page.
