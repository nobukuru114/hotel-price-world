About the data on this page

These are median prices for hotels shown as “4-star” on Booking.com that still had rooms available. The terms are two adults, one room, one night, taxes included, priced in Japanese yen. One date was sampled in each month from October 2026 to September 2027, twelve in total, collected on 2026-09-21.

Star ratings follow each country’s own conventions, so a 4-star hotel differs in facilities and location from place to place. We use 4-star as the unit of comparison because many cities list fewer than ten 5-star hotels, leaving no meaningful median, while 3-star quality varies far too much between countries.
